01 · The Sthapathi

Sthapathi
Muthu Selvan

Sthapathi Muthu Selvan is a traditional temple architect, sculptor and digital creator based in Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu, India.

A Sthapathi is a master craftsman whose work is guided by the ancient Shilpa Shastras — traditional texts defining design, rules, measurements and proportions for sculpture and temple architecture.

His practice brings together temple planning, stone carving, sculptural design, traditional detailing and on-site execution.
